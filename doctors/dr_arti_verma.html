About Dr. Arti Verma

Dr. Arti Verma (PT), BPT, is a Certified Women's Health Physiotherapy Practitioner providing specialized physiotherapy services focused on women's health and rehabilitation.


Her areas of expertise include women's health physiotherapy, pelvic floor rehabilitation, pregnancy and postnatal rehabilitation, fertility rehabilitation and sexual health physiotherapy.


Her approach focuses on individualized assessment, patient education, therapeutic exercise and functional rehabilitation tailored to the patient's needs and goals.


Home and on-call physiotherapy services are available in Jhansi. Online consultation is also available for appropriate concerns, exercise guidance and follow-up.
