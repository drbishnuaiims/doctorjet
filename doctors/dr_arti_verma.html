About

Physiotherapy focused on women's health

Individualised physiotherapy care focused on women's health, pelvic rehabilitation, pregnancy, postnatal recovery and related functional concerns.

Dr. Arti Verma (PT) is a physiotherapist with a focused practice in women's health physiotherapy and rehabilitation.

Her clinical areas include pelvic floor rehabilitation, pregnancy and postnatal physiotherapy, fertility rehabilitation and sexual health physiotherapy.

Physiotherapy programmes are tailored according to the individual's symptoms, functional goals, stage of recovery and clinical requirements.

Consultations are available through home and on-call physiotherapy services in Jhansi, along with online consultation where appropriate.
