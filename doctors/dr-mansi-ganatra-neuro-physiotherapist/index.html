About the therapist

Rehabilitation that sees the person, not just the condition.

At Raah, rehabilitation is a collaborative process—with practical goals, considered guidance, and care that respects each person's pace.

Dr. Manasi Ganatra provides specialised neurophysiotherapy for people living with neurological conditions. Treatment is planned around the person's presentation, daily activities, and rehabilitation priorities.

  • ✓Care plans tailored to individual needs and functional goals.
  • ✓Support for neurological movement and mobility challenges.
  • ✓In-clinic and online consultations by appointment.

The Raah approach

Progress is personal. The plan should be, too.

Good rehabilitation begins with listening, setting realistic goals, and building a plan around what matters in everyday life.

01 /

Understand the starting point

Consider current abilities, challenges, priorities, and the person's environment.

02 /

Work towards meaningful goals

Break functional priorities into manageable steps that can be reviewed over time.

03 /

Review and adapt

Adjust rehabilitation strategies according to response, progress, and changing needs.
