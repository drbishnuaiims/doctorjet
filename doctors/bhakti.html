About the Doctor

Healthcare with context.

Dr. Bhakti Tolamatti is a BAMS-qualified Ayurvedic consultant with additional training as a Certified Dietitian & Nutritionist and Viddha-Agnikarma Practitioner.

Her virtual practice focuses on bringing Ayurvedic principles into practical everyday healthcare — combining personalised assessment, dietary guidance, lifestyle counselling and individualised Ayurvedic care.

The emphasis is on understanding the individual, rather than treating health concerns in isolation.
