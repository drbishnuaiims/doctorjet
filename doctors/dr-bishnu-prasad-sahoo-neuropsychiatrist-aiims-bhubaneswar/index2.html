Frequently asked questions

Common appointment questions

How does the fee change?

The fee depends on visit type, duration, mode, same-day booking and priority. You see the full breakdown before you book. The clinic confirms the final amount.

What is a priority slot?

It marks your request as urgent so the clinic can handle it first. It adds 30% to the fee.

What is a report review?

A short visit to go through lab results, scans or earlier prescriptions. Please send the reports on WhatsApp once the clinic confirms.

Is online consultation available?

Yes. Online visits run on the Saheed Nagar and Puri schedules, so no clinic needs to be chosen. In-person visits are priced slightly lower.

How is my appointment confirmed?

Your details open in WhatsApp addressed to Thomson Clinic. The clinic confirms the appointment there.
