About

Focused care for skin, hair & nails

Dermatology consultation focused on common and complex skin, hair and nail concerns, with convenient online access.

Dr. Archie Kriplani is a dermatology-focused clinician providing consultation for skin, hair and nail concerns.

The practice covers concerns such as acne, pigmentation, dryness, sensitivity, ageing-related skin changes, hair fall, dandruff, scalp problems and nail disorders.

Consultation is centred on understanding the patient's concern, clinical history and relevant photographs where appropriate, followed by an individualised treatment plan.

Online consultations are available from Monday to Friday, making specialist dermatology guidance accessible without requiring an in-person visit for every concern.
