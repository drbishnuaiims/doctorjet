01 · Clinical Identity

Advanced eye care, grounded in precision.

Dr. Akshra Pahuja is a Cornea, Cataract and Refractive Surgeon with advanced training from the Dr. Rajendra Prasad Centre for Ophthalmic Sciences, AIIMS, New Delhi. 02 · Profile

A surgeon
focused on
vision.

Advanced surgical expertise, combined with a compassionate patient-centred approach.

Dr. Akshra Pahuja is a highly skilled Cornea, Cataract, and Refractive Surgeon with advanced training from the prestigious Dr. Rajendra Prasad Centre for Ophthalmic Sciences, AIIMS, New Delhi.

She completed her MBBS from University College of Medical Sciences, Delhi, followed by MD in Ophthalmology at AIIMS, New Delhi, and subsequently served as an Ex-Senior Resident in the Department of Cornea, Cataract and Refractive Services at AIIMS.

Her professional qualifications include FAICO in Refractive Surgery and the internationally recognised Certificate of the Royal College of Ophthalmologists, UK.

With over seven years of experience, her clinical work includes advanced cataract surgery, Femto-Cataract, laser vision correction, corneal transplantation, keratoconus management including CAIRS, and complex anterior segment procedures.
